The same rules on every workflow

Each workflow is built around your agency's tools and habits. These rules don't change.

  • Every output traced to a sourceEach factual line, entry or ticket links to the commit, message or ticket it came from. Anything without a source is blocked.
  • Only allowed actionsA workflow can only do the few things it's set up for, like reading a board and writing a draft. Anything else is refused and logged.
  • Internal data stays inInternal comments, private calendar events and personal details are kept out of anything that leaves your agency.
  • A person approves before anything leavesSomeone who understands the work reads the output and approves it. Nothing reaches a client, a ticket or a timesheet before that.
